Genboard 0.1

The first public build, signed and notarized by Apple.

- **Research sessions** with a built-in browser that keeps every page in the session's history, pins with screenshots and notes, dead ends, and a "where I left off" summary when you close a session.
- **A research board** of person, event, evidence and note cards, connected as parent, spouse, child, *supports* or *contradicts*, with notes on connections and a drag handle to connect cards.
- **Competing theories** with a status, evidence for and against, and their own to-dos.
- **Checks** for impossible dates and ages, run locally.
- **Family tree** charts (ancestors and descendants) and **GEDCOM export**.
- **Scapple import**.
- **Claude**, with your own Anthropic API key: questions about your research, transcribing records, and suggested cards.

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. Genboard is in early development, so expect rough edges.

Homebrew: `brew install --cask patbonecrusher/tap/genboard`
