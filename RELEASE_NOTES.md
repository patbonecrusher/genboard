Genboard 0.2

- **Click a connection line** on the board to edit it: what it means, its direction, a label, a note, and its theories.
- **Citations and source quality.** Evidence cards hold a full citation and repository, and are rated original, derivative or authored, with primary or secondary information. Each theory shows the weight of its evidence.
- **Import a GEDCOM family tree** from FamilySearch, Ancestry or other software, laid out on the board with parents above children.
- **Research reports.** Export a whole project or one theory as PDF, Markdown or a web page (⇧⌘R).
- **Search all research** across every project: pages, notes, transcriptions, cards, citations, theories and to-dos (⇧⌘F).
- **Import to Board** (File menu, ⇧⌘I): bring in a Scapple board or a GEDCOM file, or drag either onto the board.
- **Fixed:** Scapple boards with large images failed to import with "NSXMLParserErrorDomain error -1".
- **A local model.** Run questions and session summaries on a free model on your Mac with MLX; nothing leaves the computer.
- **Backups.** Export a project or all of them as a `.genboard` file and restore it on any Mac (File menu).
- **Automatic updates.** Genboard now checks for updates and installs them when you agree (Genboard ▸ Check for Updates…).

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later, on Apple silicon or Intel.

Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask genboard`
