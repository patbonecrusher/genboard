Genboard 0.1.1

- The app's bundle ID is now `com.patlaplante.Genboard`. On first launch, Genboard moves the built-in browser's sign-ins and your preferences over from the old ID, so you stay signed in to FamilySearch and the other sites. Your research is unaffected.
- If you saved an Anthropic API key, macOS may ask once whether Genboard can use it from the keychain. Choose Always Allow.

Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask genboard`
