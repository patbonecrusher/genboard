Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

In short: Genboard keeps your research on your Mac, and the developer never receives your data or anything about how you use the app. Information leaves your Mac only when the built-in browser loads a website you visit, or when you use an AI feature, which sends your request to Anthropic with your own API key.

Who this covers

This policy describes how the Genboard application for macOS ("Genboard", "the app") handles information. Genboard is developed and maintained by an individual developer ("the developer").

Information the developer collects

None. Genboard has no accounts, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising, and it never sends anything to the developer.

This data is not synced or shared. Genealogy research often includes information about living people; it stays wherever you keep your Mac's files and backups.

Websites you visit

The built-in browser loads the websites you open, such as FamilySearch, WikiTree, Find a Grave and Généalogie Québec, the same way Safari would. Those sites receive what any browser sends them, and their own privacy policies apply. Genboard doesn't send them anything else.

AI features (Claude)

The AI features are off until you add your own Anthropic API key, and they send something only when you use them: when you ask the assistant a question, transcribe a record, ask for suggested cards, or draft a session summary. Each request goes directly from your Mac to Anthropic's API, with your key, and can include:

  • your question;
  • the project's research context: its name, session goals and earlier summaries, to-dos, pinned pages with their notes, selected text and transcriptions, dead ends, and the cards, connections and theories on the board;
  • the current page's text or a screenshot of it, if you choose to attach them;
  • for transcription and suggested cards, the screenshot of that record.

Anthropic processes these requests under its own commercial terms and privacy policy, which apply to your API account. The developer never sees these requests or their answers.

Third parties

Apart from the websites you visit and, if you use them, Anthropic's API, Genboard shares nothing with anyone. It contains no third-party code.

Children

Genboard does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Genboard's handling of information changes, this page will be updated before the release that changes it, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through GitHub Issues.
